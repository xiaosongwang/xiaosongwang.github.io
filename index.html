Brief Bio

I am a Research Scientist in AI for Science at Shanghai Artificial Intelligence Laboratory, working on large-scale medical image/text pre-training and its clinical applications, as well as the Scientific Discovery Platform and Automated Laboratory. Previously, I briefly worked as a Senior Staff Algorithm Engineer at Alibaba DAMO Academy. Before moving back to China, I was a Senior Applied Research Scientist at NVIDIA (2018–2021) and a Visiting Research Fellow in Dr. Ronald Summers's CAD lab at the NIH Clinical Center (2015–2018). From 2011 to 2015 I worked at Shanghai United Imaging Healthcare (UIH), first as CAD Department Manager / Algorithm Engineer and then as Product Manager of multi-modality post-processing workstations. I received my Ph.D. in Computer Science under the supervision of Prof. Majid Mirmehdi at the University of Bristol, UK, in 2011.

My research interests lie in computer vision, vision and language, machine learning and deep learning, and their applications in medical imaging — from large-scale medical image pre-training and foundation models to weakly-supervised learning, lesion detection and clinical datasets.

Recruiting: I am looking for motivated interns, researchers and engineers at all levels. Email me if you are interested.

Research

Seven directions: from AI for Science and medical foundation models to clinical translation, datasets and federated learning.

AI4S (AI for Science)

Scientific discovery platform and automated laboratory: LLM agents that reason over wet-lab experiments — bio-experimental protocol generation with structured reward mechanisms, and large-scale LLM routing/aggregation for science workloads.

Representative papers:
  • H. Sun et al., Unleashing Scientific Reasoning for Bio-experimental Protocol Generation via Structured Component-based Reward Mechanism, ICLR, 2026.
  • S. Tang et al., Beyond Gemini-3-Pro: Revisiting LLM Routing and Aggregation at Scale, ICML, 2026.

Medical Imaging

Core medical image analysis: segmentation (2D/3D, multi-modal, multi-task), detection, weakly-supervised localization, domain generalization and AutoML — from classic CAD (lymph nodes, prostate, lung nodules) to foundation-model-era adaptations.

Representative papers:
  • Z. Tang, X. Wang, Light Yet Effective Adaptations for Multi-modal Multi-task 3D Medical Image Segmentation, ICASSP, 2026.
  • Y. Gao et al., ACDA: Anatomically constrained distribution alignment for robust medical image segmentation, Knowledge-Based Systems, 2026.
  • Y. Gao et al., A Composite Alignment-Aware Framework for Myocardial Lesion Segmentation in Cardiac MRI, MICCAI, 2025.
  • X. Wang et al., Interactive 3D Segmentation Editing and Refinement via Gated GNNs, GLMI-MICCAI, 2019. Best Paper Award

Medical Foundation Model

Large-scale medical image/text pre-training and multimodal foundation models: self-supervised pre-training, text-guided adaptation, pathology instruction learning, and medical reasoning — inference-time compute, RL-driven token-sparse reasoning, and mentor-intern collaborative search.

Representative papers:
  • K. Chen et al., Cost-effective instruction learning for pathology vision and language analysis, Nature Computational Science, 2025.
  • H. Sun et al., Chiron-o1: Igniting Multimodal LLMs towards Generalizable Medical Reasoning, NeurIPS, 2025.
  • S. Rui et al., Optimizing Inference-Time Compute for Medical Reasoning via Uncertainty Quantification, ICML, 2026.
  • Z. Luo et al., InvCoSS: Inversion-driven Continual Self-supervised Learning, IEEE CVPR, 2026.
  • X. Wang*, Y. Peng* et al., TieNet, IEEE CVPR, 2018. arXiv

Clinical Application

Translating models into clinical practice: cardiovascular risk prediction from incomplete clinical data, interpretable chest radiograph analysis, focal liver lesion diagnosis on multiparametric MRI, diabetic retinopathy detection, brain disorder diagnosis and multimodal survival analysis.

Representative papers:
  • S. Rui et al., Predicting major adverse cardiovascular events from incomplete clinical data, npj Digital Medicine, 2026.
  • L. Xu et al., A unified multi-task framework enables interpretable chest radiograph analysis, Cell Med, 2026.
  • Z. Shen et al., An Explainable Deep Learning Model for Focal Liver Lesion Diagnosis Using Multiparametric MRI, Radiology: Artificial Intelligence.
  • Z. Tang et al., Reasoning-Enhanced VLM for Interpretable Diabetic Retinopathy Detection, MICCAI-OMIA, 2025. Best Oral Paper Award
  • S. Rui et al., CardioCoT: Hierarchical Reasoning for Multimodal Survival Analysis, ICASSP, 2026.

Dataset and Benchmark

Curating large-scale public datasets and benchmarks: ChestX-ray14, DeepLesion, a multimodal retinal dataset for diabetic retinopathy, a multi-phase liver CT dataset, and a real-world benchmark for foundation model adaptation.

Federated Learning

Privacy-preserving collaborative learning across institutions: modality-agnostic heterogeneous federated learning, reinforcement-learning hyperparameter control in asynchronous federated learning, federated prostate and COVID-19 segmentation, and straggler-aware optimization.

Representative papers:
  • W. Zhang et al., Modality-Agnostic Federated Learning with Adaptive Updates for Heterogeneous Medical Image Tasks, IEEE TMI, 2026.
  • S. Gu et al., Dynamic Hyperparameter Adjustment via RL in Asynchronous Federated Learning, MICCAI-DeCaF, 2025.
  • D. Yang et al., Federated semi-supervised learning for COVID region segmentation, Medical Image Analysis, 2021.
  • H. R. Roth et al., Federated Whole Prostate Segmentation in MRI, MICCAI, 2021.
